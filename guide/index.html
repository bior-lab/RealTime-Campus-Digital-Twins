NUS Campus Digital Twins
User Guide

A concise guide to the dashboard, its data sources and its main functions.

01 About

NUS Campus Digital Twins is a web-based Digital Energy Management Information System (DEMIS) for monitoring and analysing selected NUS buildings. It combines a campus map with building energy, weather and electricity-market context.

  • Overview: explore included buildings on an interactive campus map.
  • Buildings: review monthly and yearly electricity, cooling and PV profiles.
  • Weather: compare SDE4 station observations with Kent Ridge reference weather.
  • Energy Price: view a dated market record and open the official EMC / NEMS source.

02 Data sources

DEMIS, developed by the BIOR Lab, is the platform’s data foundation. BAS and weather-station points are accessed from the PI Server through the PI Web API. Docker-based Python collectors process the records and store mapped time-series values in InfluxDB. The DEMIS API then serves selected records to the dashboard.

Point mappings connect PI names and WebIds to consistent building codes and metrics while retaining timestamp, source and unit metadata. This allows data with different origins and update frequencies to be queried by building, variable and time range.

03 Overview

The Overview maps included buildings across four campus districts. Click a district to zoom in; building labels and district colours appear at closer zoom levels.

Hover over a building for its name and available variables. Click to keep the information card open, then choose View building analysis to open its profile.

04 Buildings

In Buildings, select a building, choose Electricity, Cooling or PV, and then select a period. Availability varies by building.

PeriodDate rangeChart values
MonthlySelected calendar month (28–31 days)Daily reported energy totals, shown as a line
YearlyYear to dateMonthly energy totals

Yearly is the default. Monthly uses the most recent month with usable data. Hover over a point or bar to inspect its date and value.

Missing is not zero. Gaps and partial periods can reduce totals. Unsupported selections show No reported data.

05 Energy Price

Energy Price shows a dated local record of USEP, demand, solar output and VCP. Use View live data on EMC / NEMS for current official information.

Wholesale market values provide context only; they are not the NUS tariff or an electricity-bill estimate.

06 Campus Weather

Weather opens with the SDE4 Weather Station from DEMIS. Choose NUS Kent Ridge for Open-Meteo reference weather. Times are shown in SGT.

PeriodDate rangeChart values
WeeklyTrailing 7 daysA sample every 3 hours
MonthlyTrailing 30 daysDaily averages
YearlyCurrent calendar year, to the latest available historical dateMonthly averages of daily values

SDE4 provides separate temperature, humidity, wind and solar charts. Kent Ridge combines temperature and humidity and also shows estimated WBGT and wind direction. Hover over a chart to inspect values.

SDE4 humidity uses the current display conversion from its raw signal, wind direction is normalised to 0–360°, and WBGT is an estimate.

07 Interpretation

  • Compare the same variable, unit, period and data coverage.
  • Energy (kWh or MWh) and power (kW or MW) are not interchangeable.
  • Check the source and timestamp; an old or missing value is not a new observation or a zero.
  • Weather and market context can support analysis but do not prove why consumption changed.
