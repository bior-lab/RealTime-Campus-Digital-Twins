NUS Campus Digital Twins: A real-time energy management and information system (EMIS) for campus energy analysis.

Project Summary

NUS Campus Digital Twins integrates campus building information with electricity consumption, cooling demand and solar generation data to support energy monitoring and analysis across selected NUS buildings.

The platform brings together interactive maps, historical energy profiles, local weather conditions and Singapore wholesale electricity market information, helping users explore campus energy performance and understand its broader context.

For more information, please refer to the User Guide.
