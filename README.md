# NUS Campus Digital Twins

NUS Campus Digital Twins is a web-based energy management and information dashboard developed by the Building Informatics & Operations Research (BIOR) Lab at the National University of Singapore. The platform brings together campus building information, energy data, weather conditions and Singapore electricity-market information to support the exploration and analysis of energy performance across selected NUS buildings.

Campus operational data is provided through **DEMIS — Digital Energy Management Information System**, which collects and maps building and weather records for use by the dashboard.

## Functions

- **Interactive campus map** — explores the NUS Kent Ridge campus in 3D, identifies supported buildings by district and opens individual building analysis.
- **Building energy analysis** — presents available electricity, cooling and solar-generation data as monthly curves and yearly bar charts.
- **Energy price context** — displays EMC/NEMS electricity-price, system-demand and solar products in chart and table views.
- **Campus weather** — presents SDE4 Weather Station data from DEMIS and Open-Meteo reference weather for NUS Kent Ridge across weekly, monthly and yearly periods.
- **Data health** — shows building coverage, available variables, source freshness and missing or partial data.
- **User guide** — explains the dashboard, data sources, DEMIS framework and how to interpret each section.
